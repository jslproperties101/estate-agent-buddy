BUDDY WALK: STREETS (3D)
1. Opens full screen, the advert at the bottom disappears. Start walking.
2. Left thumb walks, right thumb looks. Both at once feel smooth.
3. Gold arrow points to the nearest sign; metres count down.
4. Next to a sign, "Price this listing" -> 3 prices. Right = points + streak; wrong = streak resets. Sign turns PRICED.
5. You cannot walk through houses or towers.
6. After 2 minutes (or Pause -> Finish round) the result card shows your score; best score kept.

VIEWING RUSH (3D)
1. Left / right pads steer, Brake slows. Car drives itself.
2. Follow the arrow to the gold beacon: points + 10 seconds.
3. Hit a building fast: "Bump!" and the car slows. Grass is slower than road.
4. Clock reaches 0 -> result card.

OPEN HOUSE TOUR (3D)
1. Walk every room; no walking through walls or furniture.
2. Tap a gold sparkle on the buyer's list: +100 and a tick.
3. Tap one NOT on the list: -5 seconds, sparkle turns red.
4. All 3 found: +15 seconds and a new buyer.
5. Sparkles on balcony, kitchen island, wardrobe, windows, door lock, aircon, study desk and bed are easy to tap.

MATCH THE BUYER
1. Swipe right/left and the Match/Skip buttons both work; Match and Skip are on screen without scrolling.
2. A wrong answer shows the reason (e.g. "Over budget by RM 40,000") and takes 5 seconds.
3. New buyer after every 6 listings.
